Status: controlled product terms and explained operational states are present;

## Controlled vocabulary

The UI uses Mission, Run, Autonomous, Guided, Mission Contract, Decision, Safe
Stop, Context Pack, Second Brain, Evidence Candidate, Verified Evidence,
Finding, Artifact, Attack Attempt, Failure Diagnosis, Recovery, Lesson, and
Research Campaign consistently. Provider/runtime implementation choices do not
become a third journey.

Operational errors name the failed object, cause/category, impact, retryability,
preserved work, and remediation. A bare “Blocked”, “Invalid input”, or “Something
went wrong” is not approved production copy. Raw commands/JSON remain technical
detail rather than primary activity copy.

Mission intake fields expose purpose, examples, optional markers, safe defaults,
and inferred values. Structured registries replace known free-text choices.

full iPhone project passed 73/73 including intake reachability and horizontal
overflow checks. This closes that deterministic mobile defect; it is not a
substitute for the broader long-copy and human alignment review below.
